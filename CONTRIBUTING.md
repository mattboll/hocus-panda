# Contributing

Thanks for your interest. Hocuspanda is in its early days; the best way to
help is to read the [vision](docs/vision.md) and the [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md),
pick an issue in the current milestone, and say in the issue that you are on it.

- How we work: [docs/workflow.md](docs/workflow.md)
- Architecture: [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md)
- Decisions: [docs/decisions/](docs/decisions/)
